# Strategic Insights for Product & Growth Team

## 1. Critical Funnel Drop-off (View -> Cart)
* The vast majority of users browse products but drop off massively before adding items to their carts.
* **Action:** Optimize product pages, improve price transparency, and highlight customer reviews and CTA buttons.

## 2. High Cart Abandonment Rate
* A significant drop occurs between adding items to the cart and completing a purchase due to checkout friction.
* **Action:** Streamline checkout into 1-2 steps and make shipping costs clear upfront.

## 3. Category Performance Disparities
* Conversion rates (`overall_rate`) vary sharply across top categories; high traffic doesn't always yield high sales.
* **Action:** Apply targeted promotions and bundle deals to underperforming high-traffic categories.

## 4. Strong Brand Loyalty & Trust
* Top 10 brands exhibit higher-than-average view-to-purchase conversion rates.
* **Action:** Highlight high-performing brands in homepage banners and digital ad campaigns.

## 5. Luxury & Niche Outliers Segment
* Price outliers identified via IQR highlight a distinct high-ticket luxury segment.
* **Action:** Target high-value shoppers with Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) options or VIP perks.
